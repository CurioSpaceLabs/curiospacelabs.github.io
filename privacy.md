### 3. Categories of Data Processed / Kategori Data yang Diproses

**[EN]** We do **not** collect, store, or process any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or sensitive personal data through your regular visits to this website.

We utilize **Cloudflare Web Analytics** to measure general website traffic. The metrics collected are purely technical and aggregated, which may include:
* Page views, loading speeds, and performance metrics
* Referrer URLs (websites visited immediately prior to arriving here)
* Technical details (browser type, operating system, device category)
* Approximate geographical location (country/region level, determined in-memory without storing full IP addresses)

**What we do NOT collect:**
* No cookies, local storage, or session storage are written to your browser for tracking.
* No browser fingerprinting or cross-site tracking across other websites.
* No full IP addresses or personal identity markers are logged or stored.

**[ID]** Kami **tidak** mengumpulkan, menyimpan, atau memproses Data Pribadi (Personally Identifiable Information/PII) maupun data spesifik/sensitif pengunjung melalui kunjungan standar ke situs web ini.

Kami menggunakan **Cloudflare Web Analytics** untuk mengukur statistik lalu lintas situs web secara umum. Data yang dikumpulkan bersifat teknis dan agregat, yang meliputi:
* Jumlah tayangan halaman (page views) dan kecepatan pemuatan situs
* URL Perujuk (situs web yang dikunjungi sebelum sampai ke situs ini)
* Informasi teknis (jenis peramban/browser, sistem operasi, kategori perangkat)
* Perkiraan lokasi geografis (sebatas tingkat negara/wilayah, diproses tanpa menyimpan alamat IP penuh)

**Yang TIDAK kami kumpulkan:**
